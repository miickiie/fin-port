# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

FinPort is a Mickey-only personal tool. Its primary user is Mickey, checking and maintaining a private investment portfolio from a local browser session.

## Product Purpose

FinPort is a local-first personal investment portfolio tracker for target allocation, investment logging, and Thai RMF/ThaiESG unlock visibility. It helps Mickey understand allocation drift, invested cost, remaining target amount, holdings history, and tax-wrapper timing without requiring a backend or brokerage connection.

RMF and ThaiESG unlock calculations are advisory planning aids only. They should not be treated as tax, legal, or financial advice.

## Positioning

FinPort is a local-first personal portfolio cockpit that helps Mickey see allocation drift, investment history, and Thai RMF/ThaiESG unlock timing without turning finances into a brokerage-style trading terminal.

## Brand Personality

FinPort should feel like a playful personal dashboard: private, clear, and encouraging without becoming unserious about money.

## Anti-references

FinPort should not look or feel like a corporate banking dashboard or a crypto/trading terminal.

## Design Principles

- Keep the portfolio story visible at a glance: allocation, progress, and unlock timing should be easy to read without digging.
- Make updates feel light: adding logs, adjusting targets, and checking settings should stay quick and low-friction.
- Preserve local-first confidence: the interface should make private browser storage, backup, and optional sync behavior understandable.
- Treat tax-wrapper information as planning context, not authoritative advice.
- Let playful details support focus, not distract from the numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target is required because this is a local personal tool. Future design work should still keep basic readability, clear bilingual English/Thai text, and non-disruptive motion in mind so the app remains comfortable for Mickey to use.
